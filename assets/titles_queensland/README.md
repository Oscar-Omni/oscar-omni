# qld-land-regions.geojson

Queensland councils for the Titles Queensland demo's custom region map. Region property `land_region` matches the demo's land region names exactly (Brisbane, Gold Coast, Logan and Ipswich, Moreton Bay, Sunshine Coast, Wide Bay Burnett, Toowoomba and Darling Downs, Central Queensland and Mackay, North and Far North Queensland). Each feature also carries `council`.

Coastal and South East Queensland councils are assigned by name. Outback, Cape and Gulf councils go by centroid latitude: north of 22.0 S is North and Far North Queensland, 22.0 to 25.6 S is Central Queensland and Mackay, south of that is Toowoomba and Darling Downs. Built 7 Nov 2026 from au-lga.geojson (see assets/australian_clinical_labs/README.md).
